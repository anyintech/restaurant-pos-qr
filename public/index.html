How to Test the Real-Time Workflow

Follow these steps to experience the complete multi-device roundtrip:

1

Open KDS & POS

Open Kitchen Display and Counter POS in separate browser tabs or a split screen.

2

Place Customer Order

Open Table 3 Menu, add Butter Chicken & Naan with a note, then click "Place Order to Kitchen".

3

Kitchen Cooks Food

The Kitchen screen rings a double chime, creates a ticket card, and starts timer. Click "Ready for Pickup".

4

Counter Settle & Reset

On Counter POS, Table 3 turns "Ready". Click Table 3, review 80mm thermal receipt, and click "Confirm Payment & Clear Table"!
